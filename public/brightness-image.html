Frequently asked questions

What do brightness and contrast change?

Brightness scales pixel intensities. Contrast changes how far they sit from the midtone. Large changes can clip highlights or shadows.

Do edits stack each time I press Process?

No. Each result starts from the loaded original using the current settings, so you can compare different values without compounding edits.

Does this use an AI enhancement service?

No. Adjustments run directly on image pixels inside your browser. Nothing is sent to an image-processing server.

Your files stay on your device

Image processing takes place in your browser. No account is needed. Downloads are new files; your original is not overwritten. Read our privacy policy.
