Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between flip and rotate?

Flipping mirrors the image across an axis. Rotating turns it around its center without mirroring.

Does flipping change the image size?

Pixel dimensions remain the same. The encoded file size may differ from the original.

Will text be reversed?

A horizontal flip mirrors text too. Check signs, logos and lettering before using the result.

Your files stay on your device

Image processing takes place in your browser. No account is needed. Downloads are new files; your original is not overwritten. Read our privacy policy.
