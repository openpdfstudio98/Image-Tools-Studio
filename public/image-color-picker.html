Frequently asked questions

Does a scaled preview reduce color accuracy?

The tool samples an original-resolution canvas. Your click is mapped from the displayed preview back to the corresponding source pixel.

Why can a photo contain slightly different colors nearby?

Compression, lighting, antialiasing and gradients can produce many similar pixels. Sample multiple points if you need a representative color.

Does HEX include transparency?

The displayed six-digit HEX value includes red, green and blue, not the alpha channel. Fully transparent pixels can have underlying RGB values that do not match the visible checkerboard.
