Frequently asked questions

Why use WebP?

WebP is a modern image format with lossy and lossless support. It can be useful for web images, but the actual saving depends on the source and encoder.

What if my browser cannot encode WebP?

The tool reports that the format is unsupported rather than silently downloading a PNG with a WebP label. Try a browser with WebP encoding or choose PNG/JPG in Compress Image.

Will dimensions change?

This converter keeps the source pixel dimensions. Use Resize Image if you also need smaller dimensions.

Your files stay on your device

Image processing takes place in your browser. No account is needed. Downloads are new files; your original is not overwritten. Read our privacy policy.
