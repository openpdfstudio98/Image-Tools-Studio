Frequently asked questions

What happens to a transparent background?

JPEG cannot store transparency. This tool places the image on a white background before encoding it.

Is JPG always smaller than PNG?

Not always. Photos often benefit from JPEG compression, while simple diagrams and small graphics can already be efficient as PNG. Compare the actual output size.

Can I choose the JPEG quality here?

This converter uses a fixed quality setting. Use Compress Image if you want to choose quality or a target KB size.
