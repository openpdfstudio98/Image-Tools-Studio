Privacy Policy

Updated 2 November 2026.

Image files

Image files, previews and processed outputs stay in your browser. Our tools do not send them to a processing server. Closing or reloading the page clears the working images. Saved downloads remain wherever you save them.

Optional usage statistics

Statistics are off until you choose Allow statistics. If allowed, the site sends only a fixed tool identifier, an action such as processing success/failure or download requested, and a count. We do not include image pixels, filenames, image dimensions, HEX values, form settings or your query string in these events. No advertising identifier, account ID or cross-site identifier is attached by our event code.

Events are handled by this website’s hosting function and written to technical logs for operational review. This release does not provide a permanent analytics database or a public visitor dashboard. The hosting provider separately handles network information such as IP addresses and standard request logs according to its service settings and policies.

Your choice

Your allow/decline preference is saved in this browser’s local storage. You can change it using Privacy choices below. Declining statistics does not disable any image tool. A browser Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal prevents our optional events.

Advertising and cookies

No advertising scripts or tracking cookies are activated in this release. If advertising is introduced, this policy and any required privacy controls will be updated before activation.

External links and contact

Opening the support tracker takes you to GitHub, which has its own privacy practices. Public reports must not include private images or sensitive information. Contact and reporting options.
