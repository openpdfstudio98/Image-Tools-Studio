How to remove a small watermark or object

  1. Choose an image and note the working resolution shown above.
  2. Adjust the brush and paint over the entire mark with a narrow margin. Undo removes the last stroke; Clear selection starts again.
  3. Select Erase selected area, inspect the result, and download the PNG. If it looks unnatural, try a smaller selection or a different source image.

Frequently asked questions

Will it perfectly remove every watermark?

No. This tool estimates selected pixels from nearby colors. It works best for small marks on simple backgrounds. Large marks, faces, lettering over textures and repeated patterns may leave blur or visible seams.

Are my images uploaded or sent to an AI service?

No. A local worker fills the selected area in your browser. No AI model or remote image service is used. Optional usage statistics do not include pixels or filenames.

What resolution will I download?

Images are fitted within 2048 pixels on each side for local processing. Smaller images keep their dimensions. The displayed working size is the PNG download size. Keep your original image as a backup.

Selection and image limits

Each selection is limited to 15% of the image or 200,000 pixels, whichever is smaller, with up to 30 brush strokes. Processing always starts from the original loaded image. This tool does not recover the hidden original content, preserve animation or guarantee original metadata.
