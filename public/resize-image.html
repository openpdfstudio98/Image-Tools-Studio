How to resize image

  1. Choose an image and check its original pixel dimensions.
  2. Enter a width or height. Preserve aspect ratio links the two dimensions; turn it off to set each independently.
  3. Process and compare the result, then download the resized image.

Frequently asked questions

Can I resize by height only?

Yes. With Preserve aspect ratio selected, edit Height and Width will follow. Editing Width works the same way.

Does resizing improve a blurry photo?

No. Enlarging creates additional pixels by interpolation; it does not recover missing detail. Use the original high-resolution image when possible.

What sizes can I export?

Output must be at most 12,000 pixels on either side and no more than 44 megapixels. Device memory may impose a lower practical limit.

Your files stay on your device

Image processing takes place in your browser. No account is needed. Downloads are new files; your original is not overwritten. Read our privacy policy.
