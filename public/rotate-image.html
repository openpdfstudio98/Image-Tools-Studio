Frequently asked questions

Does a 90-degree rotation change dimensions?

Yes. Width and height swap for 90 and 270 degrees. A 400 by 200 image becomes 200 by 400.

Can I rotate by any angle?

This version supports quarter-turn angles only. It does not provide free-angle rotation or perspective correction.

Is the original overwritten?

No. You download a separate output file. Keep your original as a backup.

Your files stay on your device

Image processing takes place in your browser. No account is needed. Downloads are new files; your original is not overwritten. Read our privacy policy.
