Frequently asked questions

Why convert WebP to JPG?

Some upload forms and older programs accept JPEG but not WebP. This converter produces a JPEG using your browser encoder.

Will animated WebP stay animated?

No. These tools export a single still image, not an animation. Keep the original file if you need motion.

Does conversion keep metadata?

Do not rely on preserving EXIF, GPS tags or other original metadata. Canvas output is a new image file.

Your files stay on your device

Image processing takes place in your browser. No account is needed. Downloads are new files; your original is not overwritten. Read our privacy policy.
